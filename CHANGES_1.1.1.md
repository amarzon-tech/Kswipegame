# K Swipe v1.1.1 — user-visible wording changes

For Dr D's sign-off. No potassium values, categories, servings, or teaching sentences were changed. This branch is `v1.1.1-fixes` only — not merged to main, and the live site is untouched.

## Wording

- Level-2 food card title **Potato, leached (boiled & drained)** is renamed **Potato, boiled and drained**. The id (`leached-potato`), serving ("½ cup diced"), potassium (~160 mg), moderate category, and teaching sentence are unchanged.
- No other card titles used "leached". Teaching sentences and the compare-card leaching explanation are unchanged (the compare title was already "Potato, boiled and drained" in 1.1).

## Other user-visible behavior (not medical wording)

- The medical footer ("Education only, not medical advice…") stays visible at 18px on every screen, including during play.
- On shorter phones, if the start-screen instructions do not all fit, a **More ↓** cue appears so the rest can be scrolled (including "Get 7 of 10 right to unlock the next level").
- In "Practice the ones you missed", the last card of a short run now says **See results** instead of **Next ➜**.
