K Swipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

We collect no data

K Swipe does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, best scores, daily streak, practice list, and sound/haptics settings are saved only in your device's local storage so the game can remember them. This information never leaves your device. You can erase it with "Reset progress & stats" on the Stats screen, or by deleting the app.

No network use

After you install the app, it works offline and does not send data over the internet.

Medical disclaimer

K Swipe is for education only and is not medical advice. Talk to your care team about your own potassium needs.
